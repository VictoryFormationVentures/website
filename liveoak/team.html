Our Standard

Live Oak Reserve began with one idea: a home should come to market at the level it was built to. A property of this caliber deserves more than photographs and a listing. It deserves a production, a plan and a team that answers the phone.

Our specialty is new construction and the land it sits on, and both are bought differently from anything else. We source lots for builders, guide custom builds and sell the finished homes, so we understand a property from the dirt up. From groundbreaking to housewarming, you deal with one team.

GJ

Gabe Jeudy-Lally

Founder, Victory Formation Ventures

Gabe grew up in Texas and spent two seasons on the Tennessee Titans' 53-man roster as a cornerback, after making the team as an undrafted free agent. The position rewards the same things this business does: preparation nobody sees, reading what is about to happen, and staying calm when it does.

He graduated from Vanderbilt and earned a master's degree from the University of Tennessee, then built his real estate career alongside Ryan Stonehouse in Nashville, working across land, development and luxury sales. He founded Victory Formation Ventures to bring that model home to Austin and the Texas Hill Country.

RS

Ryan Stonehouse

Partner

Ryan spent four seasons in the NFL as a punter, three with the Tennessee Titans and one with the Miami Dolphins. Punting is a specialist's job: a handful of moments a game, no second attempts, everything riding on preparation. He brought that habit to real estate.

He founded The Stonehouse Company in Nashville, where he leads land acquisition, development, custom home representation and luxury sales, with a practice built around new construction and the land it sits on. He is a partner in Victory Formation Ventures and works alongside Gabe on every Live Oak engagement.
