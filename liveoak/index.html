The Standard

Priced like a builder. Run like a private office.

Live Oak Reserve is the luxury real estate team of Victory Formation Ventures. We source land, develop it and represent the people who build on it, so we price a home the way a builder would: from real comps, real construction costs and what the land is truly worth.

A small team, deeply prepared. You get our cell phones, day or night, and boots on the ground from the first conversation to the closing table.

01

Honest pricing

An underwritten number you can defend, not the highest number that wins the listing.

02

Straight answers

Fees agreed in writing before anything starts. Your costs quoted up front, including the owner's title policy.

03

A plan if it stalls

A review date set on day one, with the next move agreed in advance: price, presentation or pause.

How a sale runs

Private first. Public by design.

Every launch moves through three phases, so the best buyers see a home before the portals do and the days-on-market clock never starts early.

Phase 01

Private

Shared only with The Collection and our matched buyer network. No MLS, no portals.

Phase 02

Coming soon

The film and photography go public on the home's own page and our channels, still ahead of the MLS.

Phase 03

Active

Live on the MLS and every portal with a finished campaign behind it, not a scramble.
