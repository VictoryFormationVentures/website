The Idea

A luxury brokerage brand for the homes we build and the estates we represent

Victory Formation Ventures buys land and builds homes. Live Oak Reserve is the brand those homes, and the estate and new-construction listings we represent for others, would be sold under: a curated, invitation-first marketplace for Austin's west side and the Hill Country, with an off-market list that sees inventory first.

The model borrows from the best luxury teams in other markets: a small, deeply prepared group, exceptional photography and film, honest pricing, and a private list that gets the first look. Launch depends on brokerage affiliation and Texas licensing, both in progress.

What it would offer

Private Access List

Coming-soon and off-market properties, market notes, and land opportunities, emailed to members before public marketing begins.

Sellers

Pricing built from real comps and our own build costs, launch-quality marketing, and a team that has stood on the other side of the closing table as the builder.

Buyers

Representation on estate homes and new construction, including the homes we build, with the option to design from the lot up.

Neighborhoods

Tarrytown, Westlake, Rollingwood, Lake Austin, Lakeway and Bee Cave, Dripping Springs, and the Georgetown and Leander hill country.

Builders

Back-end listing and marketing for the finished homes on lots we sourced, so a lot sale comes with a sales plan.

Past Transactions

A record of what we built and sold, by neighborhood, once the first homes close.

Working notes (remove before launch)

Name. "Live Oak Reserve" leans on Austin's signature tree and the private-list idea. Alternatives if you want to test: Limestone Collective, Hill Country Reserve, Barton Creek Private, The Formation Collection.

What has to be true to launch. Texas real estate licensing for whoever represents clients, a sponsoring broker or brokerage affiliation (Compass is the Eden Grove model; Austin options include Compass, Kuper Sotheby's, Moreland, Douglas Elliman), a photography and film partner, and an email platform for the Private Access list.

How the list would work. The form above can post straight into a "VFV Private Access" Google Sheet the same way the dashboard forms do, so signups are captured from day one even before the brand goes public.
