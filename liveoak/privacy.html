About us

Live Oak Reserve is a real estate team of Victory Formation Ventures LLC, 3004 Webberville Road #2, Austin, TX 78702, operating as a team at Brokerage. "We" and "us" in this policy mean Live Oak Reserve and Victory Formation Ventures LLC.

Information we collect

Information you give us: your name, email, phone number, the area and kind of property you are interested in, and anything you write in a message or form.

Information collected automatically: basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, which our hosting and analytics providers may record.

How we use it

To respond to your inquiry, arrange showings, send you the private listings and market notes you asked for, and provide real estate services. We do not sell your personal information.

When we share it

With our brokerage, Brokerage, and with service providers who help us run the business, such as email, hosting and form tools, only as needed to serve you. With other parties to a transaction, such as a title company or lender, when you ask us to. When the law requires it.

Email updates

If you join The Limestone Collection or ask for updates, we may email you private listings and market notes. Every email includes a way to stop. You can also email us and we will remove you.

Text messages

We only text you if you check the consent box on a form or ask us to. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We do not share your mobile number or text consent with third parties for their marketing.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use basic cookies and privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are useful. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

Retention

We keep inquiry and client records as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and brokerage record-keeping requirements, then delete or anonymize them.

Security

Client records are stored in private, access-controlled systems. No method of storage is perfect, but we limit access to the people who need it.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or stop marketing at any time by emailing gabe@victoryformationventures.com.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: gabe@victoryformationventures.com or (737) 600-5101.
