What the numbers mean

What it costs to hold

Texas has no state income tax, and property taxes do a lot of the work instead. They vary by county, city, school district and special districts, and they can change meaningfully after a sale resets the appraised value.

Before you offer, we estimate the full cost of owning the home: taxes, insurance in a market where premiums have climbed, HOA dues where they apply, and the upkeep that comes with acreage, pools and waterfront.

01

Close in vs Hill Country

Central Austin trades lot size for location. West of the city you get land, views and water, with longer drives and different tax districts. We lay out both honestly.

02

Appraised vs market

A recent purchase price can reset the taxable value. We show you what the tax bill could look like after you buy, not what the seller paid.

Areas and developments
New construction

The builder's contract is not the standard one

Many builders use their own contract or a separate new home form, and the terms favor the builder: deposits, change orders, completion dates and what happens if they slip.

We read it line by line with you, and we know which terms builders will move on and which they will not.

The honest caveat

Not every home should be bought

Part of our job is telling you when a house is wrong for you, overpriced, or carrying a problem the photos do not show. You will hear it from us before the inspection, not after.

We would rather lose a deal than put you in the wrong home.

A good price on the wrong house is still the wrong house.

Representation, money and closing

Straight answers

What representation costs

Our compensation is agreed in a written buyer representation agreement before we tour homes together, as Texas requires. The amount is negotiable and stated plainly.

Who pays it

Sometimes the seller or builder offers to cover it, sometimes it is part of your offer, sometimes you pay it directly. We structure it with you before you write an offer.

Financing and proof of funds

At the high end, sellers expect a strong pre-approval or proof of funds with the offer. We can introduce lenders who work in jumbo and construction loans.

No attorney required at closing

In Texas, closings are handled by a title company. Many buyers still have an attorney review complex contracts, and we are glad to work alongside one.
