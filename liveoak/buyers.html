Straight answers

Representation and closing

What representation costs

Texas requires a written buyer agreement before a showing. Ours states the fee plainly, and it is negotiable.

Who pays it

Often the seller or builder. If not, we structure it in your offer before you write it.

The option period

A paid window to inspect and walk away. We plan every inspection to fit inside it.

Who closes

A title company, at state-set title rates. We bring in an attorney for builder contracts and acreage.
