The tail is the market

Why a ZIP median will undersell you

In River Place and Lake Austin north the median asking price is $916,750, but the average is $2,220,426. Barton Creek and Steiner Ranch show the same shape, with averages about double the median. The homes in that top tail are the luxury market, and a headline median hides them.

That is why we never price from a ZIP code. A waterfront lot on Lake Austin, a view lot in Barton Creek and a renovated house a few streets inland can share a ZIP and still sit a million dollars apart.

Straight answers

Fees, timing and what you keep

What it costs

There is no standard commission in Texas. Our fee is agreed in writing before any work starts, and it covers the full production, not just an MLS entry. You see a net sheet with the number you keep before you sign.

Who pays the buyer's agent

Since January 1, 2026, Texas law requires every buyer to sign a written agreement with their agent before a showing, and that agreement states what the agent is paid. So you will know early whether a buyer expects help covering it.

Offering something is your choice, not a requirement. A buyer who has to fund their own agent has less to put toward your house. We model both before you decide.

The option period

Most Texas contracts give the buyer a paid option period, a short window to walk away for any reason. At this price we negotiate the length and the fee so that window is tight and the money is real.

What you pay at closing

Texas has no transfer tax, at any price. In the Austin area the seller customarily pays for the buyer's owner title policy, and title premiums are set by the state, so they are the same at every title company.

Texas property taxes are paid in arrears, so you credit the buyer for your share of the year at closing. An existing survey can often be reused with a T-47 affidavit, which saves time and money.

What you disclose

Texas requires a Seller's Disclosure Notice on most resales, including questions about flooding, flood insurance claims and the floodplain. We help you complete it fully and early. A clean, complete disclosure is a selling point.

Your privacy

Because Texas does not publish sale prices, your final number stays between you, the buyer and the MLS. We keep private exclusives private, with no public price history until you choose to go public.
