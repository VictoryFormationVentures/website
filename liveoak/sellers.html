Where the market is

Austin is many markets

The Hill Country does not move as one market. A renovated home in Tarrytown, a waterfront lot on Lake Austin and new construction in Dripping Springs each have their own buyers, timing and price per foot. We read the micro market, not the metro headline.

Inventory, days on market and the gap between list and sale price change block to block. Before we set a number we pull the closings that compare, the active competition, and what builders are paying for land nearby.

01

Close in vs Hill Country

Central neighborhoods trade on lot, location and schools. Out west, acreage, views and water access lead. We price each on its own terms.

02

The top of the market

At the high end there are fewer sales, so every comparable matters more. We know which recent closings truly compare and which ones mislead.

03

Builders as buyers

Some homes are worth more as land. Because we work with builders every week, we can tell you when that is true and who would pay for it.

The launch

Private first, public by design

Private exclusive, shared with The Limestone Collection and our matched buyer network. No MLS, no portals, no clock running.

Then coming soon, public on the home's own page and our channels, still ahead of the MLS. Then active, priced on what the first two phases taught us.

We set a review date before launch, so if it is not moving the next step is already agreed: price, presentation or pause.

Straight answers

Fees, timing and what you keep

Our fee

Our fee is agreed in writing before anything starts. It is negotiable, as every listing fee is, and it covers the full production, not just the MLS entry.

Buyer agent compensation

That is your decision. Since the 2024 industry changes, compensation for a buyer's agent is negotiated separately and is not required. We will walk through the trade-offs with you.

How a private launch works

A defined window, agreed up front, where the home is shown privately before it goes public. You can move to the open market at any time we both agree it is right.

Your costs at closing

Texas has no state transfer tax. In the Austin area, sellers customarily pay for the buyer's owner title policy, plus their own share of fees and prorated taxes.

We give you a net sheet before you list, so the number you keep is never a surprise.
