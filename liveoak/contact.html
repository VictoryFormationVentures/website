Questions
What is Live Oak Reserve?

Live Oak Reserve is the luxury real estate team of Victory Formation Ventures, a real estate firm working across land acquisition, development, custom home representation and sales in the Texas Hill Country. The team is led by Gabe Jeudy-Lally with partner Ryan Stonehouse.

What areas do you cover?

The Texas Hill Country: Travis, Williamson and Hays counties. That includes central Austin neighborhoods like Tarrytown, the west side from Westlake to Barton Creek, the Lake Austin and Lake Travis corridors, and the Hill Country towns beyond them such as Dripping Springs and Georgetown.

What kind of properties do you represent?

Estate homes, waterfront and acreage properties, and new construction, including homes we helped bring from raw land to finished house. We represent sellers and buyers, and we work with builders and landowners every week.

What is The Limestone Collection?

Our private list. Members see private exclusives, market notes and opportunities before they reach the open market. Requests are reviewed personally, and you can leave the list at any time.

What makes your marketing different?

As a production: film, architectural photography, drone, a website of its own and targeted reach, launched in three phases, private, then coming soon, then active. See the marketing page for the full approach.

Do you work with builders and developers?

Yes. We source land that fits each builder's criteria, keep every buy box private, and market and sell the finished homes. Builders never compete against each other through us.

Can you help me build a custom home?

Yes. We can help you find the lot, assemble the right builder and architect, and represent your interests through the build, from groundbreaking to housewarming.

How do I reach you?

Call or text (737) 600-5101, email gabe@victoryformationventures.com, or use the form above. We reply personally.
