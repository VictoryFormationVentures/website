Our Mission

Our mission is to turn well-chosen ground into homes people are proud of. We buy lots and acreage in Travis, Williamson, and Hays counties, carry them through zoning, entitlement, and design, and deliver finished homes with the preparation and market command that Central Texas real estate demands. Strategy, counsel, and follow-through are the work; the houses are the proof.

Services

Six ways we work

01 · Land Acquisition

We buy lots and acreage

Infill lots, teardowns, and tracts from a quarter acre to a hundred. We underwrite quickly, close on our own timeline, and pay fairly for land with a story: odd shape, slope, floodplain, or an estate to settle. If you own land in the Austin area and want a clean exit, start with us.

02 · Development

From dirt to doorstep

Zoning research, platting, utilities, entitlement, and vertical construction, managed as one process. We know which city is which (Austin's HOME rules, Leander's composite zoning, Buda's form districts) and we build the pro forma before we build the house.

03 · Custom Home Representation

Your advocate on your build

Building a custom home is a two-year, seven-figure project with a dozen counterparties. We represent the owner: architect and builder selection, budget and schedule control, draw reviews, and site visits, so the person paying for the house has someone in every meeting who works for them.

04 · Brokerage

Sell to us, then let us find your next one

Most people selling land or a house need somewhere to go. When you sell to us, we can represent you as your buyer's agent on the next home, and the two closings can be timed together so you're never carrying two mortgages or scrambling for a rental. One conversation, both sides handled.

05 · Selling

Listing and marketing for luxury spec builders

Finished homes deserve a launch, not a listing. For the builders we work with, we handle the sale on the back end: pricing strategy, professional photography and film, a private preview to our buyer and agent network before the home goes public, and negotiation through closing. The lot we sold you comes with a sales plan.

06 · Investment

Partner on the upside

For builders who want lots without carrying land, and for capital partners who want Austin residential exposure with a hands-on operator, we structure lot programs, joint ventures, and single-project investments with clear returns and clean reporting.

Approach

Preparation is the edge

Every parcel we consider gets the same treatment before we make an offer: parcel and frontage from the county appraisal district, the city's actual zoning district and what it allows, FEMA and City of Austin floodplain, slope from LiDAR, and a call to the planning desk. That preparation is why we can move fast and why our offers hold.

We are builders first. The land we buy is land we intend to build on, which means we look at it the way a family will eventually look at it: where the driveway goes, where the light comes from, what the neighbors see.
