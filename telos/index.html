Why Telos

Everyone works for someone

Telos (τέλος): the end goal, the purpose

A custom home is a two-year, seven-figure project with a dozen counterparties: architect, builder, lender, city, inspectors and trades. Each of them answers to someone. Telos answers to you.

We keep the vision, the budget and the schedule pointed at the home you set out to build, and we are in the room for every decision that moves them.
